# Product Vision

## Overview

AI Nexus Block is an Agentic Knowledge OS and Multi-Tenant Developer Sandbox. It is a long-term AI-powered platform that helps developers and students discover AI tools, learn technologies, document projects, debug code, and maintain a living developer portfolio.

## Problem Statement

Developers and students face an overwhelming landscape of AI and developer tools. There is no single platform that combines tool discovery, knowledge management, project documentation, AI-assisted debugging, and portfolio maintenance into one cohesive experience.

## Solution

AI Nexus Block provides:

1. **Tool Discovery** — A curated catalog of AI and developer tools with pricing, categories, and AI-powered recommendations.
2. **Knowledge Platform** — Articles, guides, and engineering insights with search and categorization.
3. **Engineering Roadmaps** — Structured learning paths from idea to production.
4. **Project Showcase** — A living portfolio with case studies, technologies, and links.
5. **AI Assistant** — Multi-provider AI assistant for stack recommendations, debugging, tool comparison, project planning, and concept learning.
6. **Engineering Journal** — Debug sessions, notes, conversations, and engineering decision records.
7. **Developer Journey** — A timeline of milestones and achievements.

## Target Users

- Developers exploring AI tools and technologies
- Students learning engineering and AI concepts
- Engineers documenting projects and decisions
- Technical interview candidates preparing with AI assistance

## Two Experiences

### Public / Visitor Experience
Visitors can browse all public content, interact with the AI assistant, and explore the developer's portfolio without authentication.

### Authenticated / Admin Workspace
The owner/admin can manage all content, configure AI settings, manage media, view analytics, and use the full workspace (assistant, debug, notes, conversations, decisions).

## Future Direction

- Multi-tenant support for multiple developers
- RAG-powered knowledge search with pgvector
- AI-powered tool comparison and recommendation engine
- Analytics dashboard with traffic and engagement metrics
- Full CMS with draft/publish workflow
